Registry 性能基准测试#

测试前设置#

export REG_URL="192.168.3.111:5000/perf"
export BASE_IMG="alpine:latest"

generate_image.sh 会在当前目录生成 spec-<SIZE>gb/ 构建目录。大规格测试后及时清理：

rm -rf spec-*gb/

1GB#

./generate_image.sh 1
time docker push 192.168.3.111:5000/perf/spec-1gb:v1
  • push: 5s
  • pull: 8s
